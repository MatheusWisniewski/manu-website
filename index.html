01 — About me

Paediatric care meets infection control.

I trained as a nurse at Faculdades Pequeno Príncipe in Curitiba, Brazil, and cared for children of all ages at the Pequeno Príncipe Hospital, the largest paediatric hospital complex in Latin America.

Moving into infection control, I led active surveillance for healthcare-associated infections. I also helped put prevention policies in place and delivered evidence-based training to multidisciplinary teams.

Since moving to London, I have spent two years at an international organisation, which sharpened my communication, adaptability and professional English. I'm also completing an MSc in Control of Infectious Diseases at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine (expected March 2027).

02 — Expertise

What I bring to the ward.

❤️

Direct patient care

Holistic care from neonates to adolescents, including pre- and post-operative care, vital signs, fluid balance and spotting clinical deterioration early.

🧼

HAI surveillance, compliance audits, antimicrobial stewardship and applied clinical microbiology to keep patients and staff safe.

Teamwork & education
